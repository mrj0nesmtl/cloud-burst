## 📝 **Overview**  
This **Statement of Work** outlines the **production requirements** for Cloud Burst, an **AI-powered event photography platform**.  

📌 *The project is structured into three phases:*  
1️⃣ **The Foundation** – Setup, infrastructure, and integrations (100% Complete)  
2️⃣ **The Features** – Core frontend & backend development (40% Complete)  
3️⃣ **Deployment & Launch** – CI/CD pipelines, testing, and go-live (Planned)

## 📧 **Email Template Management System**

### 🎯 **Core Functionality**
✅ **Template Database** – Structured storage with RLS policies
✅ **Template Editor** – HTML and subject line editing
✅ **Template Preview** – Real-time rendering with variable substitution

✅ **API Layer** – Secure endpoints for template management

✅ **API Protection** – Authenticated endpoints
